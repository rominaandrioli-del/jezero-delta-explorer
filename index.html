EVA-01: Jezero Delta Explorer

Plan, explore, and make scientific decisions during a simulated astronaut traverse through Jezero Crater.

BEGIN EXPLORATION

Mission Overview

Explore Jezero Crater through the eyes of future Mars astronauts. Investigate an ancient river delta, study sedimentary rocks, select valuable scientific samples, and prepare them for future return missions.

Mission Objectives

  • Study the Jezero river delta
  • Investigate sedimentary rock formations
  • Evaluate potential sampling sites
  • Preserve samples for future missions
  • Plan a safe exploration route
